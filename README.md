# 🌿 Growing Focus

A cozy, single-file Pomodoro-style focus timer. Every focus session you complete grows a flower in your garden.

## Features

- Pick a focus duration — 15, 25, or 50 minutes
- Watch a flower grow as your session progresses
- Give up early and the flower wilts (no cheating!)
- Tracks your streak, total flowers grown, and total focused minutes
- Everything is saved locally in your browser (`localStorage`) — no account or server needed

## Usage

Just open `index.html` in any modern browser. No build step, no dependencies.

## Tech

Plain HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript in a single file.
